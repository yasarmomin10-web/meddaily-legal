MedDaily Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

1. Who we are

MedDaily is a pharmacy billing and stock app published by Umarfarkhund Ayubali Sayyed ("we", "us"). Contact: srhbusiness2420@gmail.com.

2. Information the app handles

The app stores your pharmacy records (medicines, stock, customers, suppliers, bills, purchases, payments and settings) on your own phone or computer. We do not run a server that receives or stores this data, and we cannot see it. The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

3. Google sign-in (optional)

If you tap "Connect Google", the app asks you to sign in with your Google account and requests these permissions:

  • Email address: to check that you signed in with the Gmail you entered. The email you type is kept only on your own device.
  • Google Drive (drive.file): lets the app create and manage only the files that the app itself creates, namely your MedDaily backup files and the "MedDaily Data" spreadsheet. The app cannot see your other Drive files.

4. How your information is used

Your records are copied only to your own Google Drive, as a backup file and as a Google Sheet, so that you can restore your data and view it in a spreadsheet. The app uses Google data only to provide these backup, restore and spreadsheet features. This data goes directly between your device and your Google account. We do not receive a copy.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data, do not share it with anyone, and do not use it for advertising. MedDaily's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

When you choose to send a bill or a reminder through WhatsApp, the message text is passed to WhatsApp only because you tapped that button. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to it. The app also loads fonts from Google Fonts.

6. Storage and security

Your Google sign-in token is stored on your device. Backups and spreadsheets are stored in your own Google account and are protected by your Google account security. We recommend a screen lock on your device and the app PIN.

7. Your choices

  • Tap "Disconnect" in the app Settings to stop the connection.
  • You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You can delete the backup files and the spreadsheet from your Google Drive at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the records stored on that device.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.

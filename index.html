MedDaily

MedDaily is a billing and stock management app for medical stores and pharmacies. It runs on Android phones and Windows computers and keeps your records on your own device.

If you choose, you can connect your own Google account so the app saves a backup copy and a spreadsheet of your records in your own Google Drive.
